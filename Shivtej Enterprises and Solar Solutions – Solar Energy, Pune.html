Common questions

Quick answers before you call.

How much roof space do I need?

As a rule of thumb, about 100 sq ft of shadow-free roof for every 1 kW of solar. We measure your roof at the site visit.

Does solar work in the monsoon?

Yes. Panels produce less on cloudy days, but they keep generating. Over the year the system is sized to cover your needs.

Do solar panels need maintenance?

They need regular cleaning and an occasional performance check. We offer maintenance and repair for both our own and other installers’ systems.

Do you work outside Pune?

Yes. Pune is our base, and we take up installation and maintenance projects all over India.
